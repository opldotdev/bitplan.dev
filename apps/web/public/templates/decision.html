Shortcuts leave normal typing alone. Move and resize your annotations with their corner handles. On touch screens, use the annotation picker in + New version.

Live is not published

Edits and notes are encrypted in the shared room. They do not advance the plan’s version number. Your agent saves a new hosted version only after you review it.

Publish on chain in the revision panel changes the request in your prompt. It does not sign or spend. A permanent publication needs its own review and wallet approval.

Author-owned annotation checkpoint publishing is not yet connected end to end. Do not mistake a live note, avatar, or copied prompt for a verified on-chain record.
